## What the 9 quarters show

### 1. Headcount: real compression, partial recovery in Q4

```
2024-Q1: 1,441k  baseline (FY25 start, pre-reduction peak)
2024-Q2: 1,425k  -16k  (hiring freeze becomes visible)
2024-Q3: 1,415k  -10k
2024-Q4: 1,405k  -10k
2025-Q1: 1,415k  +10k  (small recovery)
2025-Q2: 1,400k  -15k
2025-Q3: 1,392k  -8k
2025-Q4: 1,383k  -9k   ← peak-to-trough
2026-Q1: 1,399k  +16k  (Q4 FY26 confirmed; partial recovery)
```

**Peak-to-trough**: -58k headcount over 7 quarters (~4% reduction).
**Q4 FY26 reversal**: +16k from trough — TCS confirmed 584,519 (up from 582,163 in Q3); Wipro confirmed 242,156.
**Read**: The cumulative compression is real. The Q4 reversal is small (1.2% of total) and could be either (a) institutional friction limiting AI shock per citadel, or (b) noise within an ongoing decline trend per citrini. Inconclusive on its own — needs Q1 FY27 confirmation.

### 2. Revenue per employee (TCS): clean +14% over 2 years

```
2024-Q1: $45.8k
2024-Q4: $47.5k   (FY25 +3.7% YoY)
2025-Q4: $51.0k   (FY26 +7.4% YoY)
2026-Q1: $52.2k   (Q4 FY26 confirmed)
```

**Acceleration in last 4 quarters**: +9% vs +5% in first 4 quarters of the window.
**Read**: Same workforce shrinking, output per worker rising faster. Consistent with both theses but the *acceleration* in productivity-per-head matters: it's not just "fewer employees, same output" (citadel-friendly), it's "fewer employees, MORE output" (more aligned with citrini's substitution thesis at the firm level).

### 3. TCS AI revenue mix: 3× growth, linear path

```
2024-Q3:  2.5%  (AI revenue nascent, ~$0.7B annualized)
2025-Q1:  3.8%
2025-Q2:  5.0%
2025-Q4:  6.8%
2026-Q1:  7.5%  (Q4 FY26 confirmed; $2.3B annualized / $30.5B total)
```

**Pattern**: Linear acceleration, +0.5-1pp per quarter. From $0.7B → $2.3B annualized in 6 quarters.
**Read**: Customers are buying AI-delivered services at scale. This is real diffusion. Linear (not exponential) suggests institutional procurement pace, not viral adoption — a citadel-flavored speed but in a citrini-flavored direction (the AI revenue replaces traditional engineer-hours).

### 4. Wipro utilization: steady creep upward

```
2025-Q1: 82.0%
2025-Q3: 82.5%
2025-Q4: 83.2%
2026-Q1: 84.0%  (Q4 FY26 confirmed; band 83.5-84.5%, midpoint)
```

**+2pp in 5 quarters.**
**Read**: Even with shrinking headcount, the workers staying are billable more hours. Productivity gain at the worker level. Consistent with both theses but specifically supports "AI complements remaining workers" (citadel) IF combined with stable hiring; supports substitution if combined with continued headcount decline.

## What this dataset cannot answer

The India IT proxy is strong for **diffusion + productivity + labor** modules but silent on:
- **demand** (US discretionary spending, restaurant/travel) — needs separate ingest from BLS, Census retail trade
- **intermediation** (SaaS pricing pressure, build-vs-buy mentions) — needs corporate text sources
- **credit_housing** (HELOC draws, delinquencies, home prices) — needs Fed/MBA/CoreLogic data

For the citrini thesis to be confirmed at macro level, *spillover* from labor module to demand → intermediation → credit_housing is required. India IT data shows the *origin shock* (labor + productivity) but cannot tell us whether spillover is happening.
